Hafiz Hilal Ahmad is a structural engineer and MS candidate at NUST Islamabad, combining hands-on experimental research on the seismic behaviour of confined masonry with an industry background in large-scale infrastructure construction. His thesis investigates the dynamic performance of a confined asymmetrical masonry building through shake-table testing and finite-element analysis. He is motivated to pursue doctoral research in earthquake engineering and the performance-based seismic assessment of masonry structures, particularly in low- and middle-income regions.
